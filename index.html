About Hello Burger

Our History

2018

It started selling on a three-wheeled cart at the night market in Sagaing. It was a small business started by a single person, with no background, when the founder was just over 18 years old.

2019

Instead of selling from a three-wheeled cart, they sold their products as a counter shop on the side of the road to make it more organized. During that time, they also gained the trust of consumers.

2020

The business was renamed Ko Pee Shop. Due to the increasing support from consumers, a shop with a seating area for about 15 customers was opened near the nine-foot bridge, undergoing renovations after an earthquake.

2022 & 2023

After opening a small shop named Hello Food & Drink near Mandalay Lake in 2022, the store was expanded to Chin Su Road in Sagaing City under the official HELLOBURGER brand in 2023.

2026

Thanks to overwhelming consumer demand, we have proudly opened a new Mandalay branch.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: The most important mission is to make sure that every hungry customer is completely satisfied when they come to HELLOBURGER.

We will strive to open branches in other cities as soon as possible so that our customers can easily eat at HELLOBURGER whenever they are hungry.

Our Vision

Founded in 2019 in SAGAING, HELLOBURGER has a bold vision for the next decade and is expanding with branches in many cities in Myanmar.

HELLOBURGER is committed to becoming a company that enhances the future by supporting the growth of our employees (Team Members) step by step.

Our goal is to establish ourselves as the most successful and sustainable company in Myanmar's fast food restaurant industry and grow into a leading franchise business.
