About Us

Our Story Began With A Passion For Burgers.

Founded in 2019, HELLO BURGER started as a small street food business with a passion for serving quality burgers and creating a unique customer experience.

Today, HELLO BURGER has grown into a recognized F&B brand with trademark registration in progress, operating multiple branches across Myanmar with a strong team, organized systems, and consistent branding standards.

Franchise

Own Your HELLO BURGER Branch!

As customer demand grows, we welcome business partners who want to grow together through our structured Franchise Network.

Standard Branch

Full Dine-in Restaurant Concept

  • Area: 450-500 Sqft (Recommended 15'x30')
  • Capacity: 16-20 Customers (4-5 Tables)
  • Service: Dine-in + Take Away
  • Setup Time: 30-45 Days

Advantages:

Strong brand visibility, higher customer experience, better for long-term expansion on main roads.

Mall Branch

Compact Take Away Concept

  • Area: 80-150 Sqft
  • Type: Kiosk / Mall Counter
  • Service: Take Away Only
  • Setup Time: 14-25 Days

Advantages:

Lower initial investment, faster daily operations, suitable for small spaces like food courts and malls.
